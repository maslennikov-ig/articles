# Cover Image Prompts — Template Bridge Evolution

## Prompt 1: The Bridge (conceptual)

A minimalist dark tech illustration: three glowing pillars labeled with icons (brain, checklist, puzzle piece) connected by a luminous bridge structure. The bridge is sleek, modern, futuristic. Below the bridge — scattered broken gears and abandoned tools fading into darkness. Clean composition, cinematic lighting, purple and blue tones. Text on the image must be in Russian: "3000+ часов с AI" in bold modern font at the top.

## Prompt 2: Evolution Timeline

Split-screen composition. Left side (dark, cluttered): a tangled mess of 39 floating code blocks, agents, wires — chaos, complexity, red warning lights. Right side (clean, bright): three simple glowing cubes aligned perfectly, connected by thin light beams — minimal, elegant, blue-white tones. A large arrow in the center pointing left to right. Dramatic lighting. Text on the image must be in Russian: "От 39 агентов к трём плагинам" at the bottom in clean sans-serif font.

## Prompt 3: The Multiplier (conceptual math)

Dark background with a large glowing equation in the center: "10 × AI = 100" with a bright neon glow. Below it, dimmer: "0 × AI = 0". A silhouette of a developer at a terminal, illuminated by the screen glow. Atmospheric, moody, cyberpunk-lite aesthetic. Text on the image must be in Russian: "AI — мультипликатор" in a subtle font at the top right corner.

## Prompt 4: Three Shields (protection layers)

A futuristic holographic display showing three concentric defensive shields or barriers around a central glowing AI core. Each shield layer has a different color (gold, blue, green) and a small icon (document, bell, list). The shields are semi-transparent with data streams flowing through them. Dark space background. Cinematic, sci-fi aesthetic. Text on the image must be in Russian: "Три уровня защиты" in holographic font style at the bottom.

## Prompt 5: Terminal with Story

A dramatic close-up of a developer's desk at night. Monitor showing a terminal with glowing green text. On the screen — a visible counter showing "3247 hrs". Coffee cup with steam. Warm desk lamp creating contrast with cold monitor light. Photorealistic, shallow depth of field, cozy but intense atmosphere. Text on the image must be in Russian: "3000+ часов в Claude Code" overlaid in a modern semi-transparent banner at the top of the image.
